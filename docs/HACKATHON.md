# SanD Hacks Fall 2026: rules and context

SightLine is being built for SanD Hacks Fall 2026 at UC San Diego, 14 to 15 November 2026. This file covers the parts of the hacker guide that matter for building and submitting.

- Website: https://hackathon.csesatucsd.com
- Devpost: https://sand-hacks-26.devpost.com/
- Slack is the official channel for announcements. The invite link is in the hacker guide and is left out here because this repo is public.

## Hard rules (breaking one means disqualification)

- **No commits before Saturday 14 Nov, 10:30 AM, or after Sunday 15 Nov, 12:00 PM (Pacific).** The first commit in this repo is at 10:56 AM Saturday, so we're fine. Never commit after noon on Sunday.
- The event is in person. The whole team must be at check-in, at judging and at the award ceremony.
- Every team member must be at the table for the whole of judging (12:30 to 2:30 PM Sunday). If anyone is missing, the judge scores the project zero.
- Teams have 1 to 4 people and are locked in when we submit.
- Any AI tools and anything on the internet may be used.
- Everyone must follow the UC San Diego Principles of Community.

## What a submission needs (on Devpost)

1. **A 3-minute video**: the idea, a demo (the most important part) and the technology used. Upload it to YouTube or Google Drive and make it **public**.
2. **A short write-up**: answers to the Devpost prompts on the problem, solution, challenges and tools used.
3. **The GitHub repo link.**
4. A live demo link (optional, but we should have one).

## Judging

Judges visit tables. Each one gets **3 minutes of presentation** and then **2 minutes of questions**. How many judges we see depends on the tracks we enter. Judging is holistic:

- **Idea**: is it a real problem, and is the solution creative and practical?
- **Demo**: did it work, and does it show the project well?
- **Technical complexity**: does it use interesting technology or new algorithms?
- **Completion**: does it do everything it claims to do?

Code quality, comments and file structure are **not** judged.
